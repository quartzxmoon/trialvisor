# Tests

## Test 1 - Public trust surfaces and first-time onboarding [sanity]
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: public landing, How It Works, Security, Provider Support, Subprocessors, authentication, account initialization, resumable onboarding
Description: Verifies that public education and trust pages are reachable without authentication, make the authorization boundaries explicit, and preserve first-run account setup.

Steps:
1. Open the landing page and visit How It Works, Security, Provider Support, and Subprocessors from public navigation
2. Confirm the lifecycle reads Connect, Discover, Review, Protect, Monitor, Decide, Cancel, Verify
3. Sign in with a first-time Trialvisor identity, refresh during onboarding, then continue without connecting an inbox

Expected: Public pages render without a session; they state that inbox connection and detection do not authorize cancellation, do not advertise any provider as Autopilot, list only confirmed production dependencies, and expose a security-reporting route. The new user resumes onboarding after refresh and reaches the protection center only after completing setup.

## Test 4 - Connected Google account uses bounded, review-only discovery
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: authenticated session restoration, Google connection state, Gmail sync, enrollment-intent classification, promotional exclusion, conservative financial exposure, Protect eligibility, canonical entity resolution, bounded focused-search pagination, versioned reprocessing, incremental history pagination and checkpoint fallback, deduplication, persistent sync status
Description: Verifies the dashboard and privacy-minimized Gmail processing recover a refreshable authenticated session without treating marketing offers as active financial risk.

Steps:
1. Connect Google through production OAuth, allow the access token to require refresh, and reload the application
2. Confirm the dashboard returns, then select Sync from Connected Accounts
3. Review the result, Notification Center, and any detected finding, then sync again after another session refresh

Expected: The refreshable AppDeploy session is restored before the dashboard or Sync request and a failed restoration shows explicit sign-in recovery instead of an endless loader. Google callback success is visibly confirmed even while a session is restoring; denied or failed callbacks remain actionable, and each stale OAuth query parameter is removed after consumption. Account identity and last sync remain visible. Until the first real match, the bounded validation search can reprocess candidates handled by an older parser version and paginates within the cap; after validation, incremental history avoids unnecessary full searches and also paginates within the cap. If candidate retrieval fails, Trialvisor visibly requests a retry and preserves the prior history checkpoint instead of losing the candidate. Connected Accounts retains a privacy-safe last-sync summary after reload, including mode, scanned, active, possible, promotional, created, duplicate, and retrieval-failure counts. Offer-only messages such as “Start your free trial,” “Get 3 months free,” Xfinity free-line offers, upgrades, discounts, bundles, and retention offers create no active finding, add $0 confirmed exposure, and are not Protect eligible. Strong completed-enrollment, renewal, billing, or receipt evidence becomes ACTIVE; ambiguous ownership remains REVIEW_REQUIRED and is excluded from confirmed exposure. Processed messages store normalized signals and source references rather than full bodies. Related evidence for one provider/account/plan updates one canonical entity, repeated sync does not duplicate it, and different providers, accounts, or materially different plans are not collapsed. Detection and sync never grant cancellation authority. The result states its search mode, classification counts, confidence mix, and whether entity resolution prevented a duplicate.

## Test 6 - Canva Guided cancellation and independent Gmail evidence
Viewport: desktop (1280x800) and mobile (375x667)
Covers: truthful provider tier, explicit authorization, Action Required, user/provider evidence boundary, Gmail authentication checks, ambiguity guard, job revocation
Description: Verifies a Guided provider workflow never becomes Autopilot or Verified Cancellation from a customer self-report and accepts only narrow authenticated provider evidence.

Steps:
1. Add or discover a Canva trial and explicitly choose Protect.
2. Execute the due cancellation path.
3. Confirm it becomes persistent Action Required, is labeled Guided, and links to Canva's official cancellation instructions. Confirm it is never labeled Autopilot.
4. Select "I completed the provider steps".
5. Confirm the item remains unverified and the activity timeline records a USER ACTION only.
6. Sync connected Gmail with no authenticated Canva confirmation and confirm the state remains CANCELLATION_NEEDS_USER.
7. Verify that a spoofed sender, failed/missing Canva DKIM identity, instructional email, email predating Protect authorization, or ambiguous match across multiple eligible Canva items cannot mark a cancellation verified.
8. With one eligible protected Canva item and a later real Canva confirmation whose Gmail Authentication-Results show passing Canva DKIM, sync again.

Expected: Exactly one item becomes CANCELED_CONFIRMED; remaining scheduled jobs are revoked; the timeline labels the evidence PROVIDER RESULT; a persistent success notification appears; only the message/thread reference, normalized evidence timestamp, and verification status are retained; no complete email body is stored or returned.

## Test 7 - Stripe sandbox subscription lifecycle and tenant isolation
Viewport: desktop (1280x800) and mobile (375x667)
Covers: monthly/annual Checkout, signed webhooks, server-owned entitlement, Customer Portal, renewal, payment failure, cancellation, expiration, replay safety, customer binding
Description: Verifies Trialvisor Pro is granted only from verified Stripe test-mode subscription state and a Stripe Customer can never cross Trialvisor tenant boundaries.

Setup:
1. Use only the dedicated Trialvisor Stripe sandbox and the existing Trialvisor Pro monthly and annual Prices.
2. Attach Stripe credentials through AppDeploy private secrets.

Steps:
1. From a Free tenant, choose monthly and confirm the button sends an authenticated request with the exact Trialvisor app-session ID, displays a safe error if the request fails, and otherwise opens prebuilt Stripe Checkout. Abandon it, reopen the same Checkout without creating a duplicate, then complete it with a Stripe test payment method and return to Trialvisor.
2. Before webhook completion, attempt a second Checkout and confirm Trialvisor blocks it; then confirm the browser return alone does not grant Pro, deliver the signed subscription event before the Checkout completion event, and confirm durable Pro access. Deliver Checkout completion afterward and confirm it cannot downgrade or duplicate the entitlement.
3. Confirm the current renewal date comes from the subscription item, then open Customer Portal, cancel at period end, confirm Pro remains through the paid period, then advance the sandbox clock through expiration and confirm a safe return to Free.
4. Repeat with the annual Price and confirm Plan & Billing still shows the server-owned yearly Pro state after reload and after sign-out/sign-in. Then test renewal, payment failure, past_due/unpaid state, supported reactivation, Checkout abandonment, and an invalid Price ID. After a subscription fully ends, start a replacement Checkout and confirm the prior Stripe Customer is safely reused while prior subscription/Price/period fields cannot block or contaminate the new signed subscription event.
5. Replay an already processed webhook and send a webhook with an invalid signature.
6. As a Free tenant, attempt to Protect a review-only finding and confirm the backend rejects the new authorization. After the signed subscription event grants Pro, Protect the finding successfully; then remove the entitlement and confirm the already-authorized Keep, cancellation, worker, and verification paths remain available.
7. Attempt Checkout and Portal access while tenant A's billing record contains tenant B's Stripe Customer ID, and call both billing endpoints without authentication.

Expected: Trialvisor permits only one unresolved Checkout per tenant, preventing monthly and annual subscriptions from being created concurrently even if webhooks are delayed. An open Checkout is resumed, double clicks are idempotent, and every failure is visible rather than appearing as a dead button. Checkout creates or reuses only the authenticated tenant's Stripe Customer. Both Checkout reuse and Portal creation revalidate the global customer-to-tenant binding before contacting Stripe. Signed, allowlisted active/trialing subscription events alone grant Pro even when Stripe delivers subscription and Checkout events out of order, while unrelated Checkout metadata or a mismatched customer/subscription is rejected. The Plan & Billing page reconciles from authoritative Stripe state, prefers the active recognized subscription over old canceled subscriptions, and never silently presents purchase controls while state is pending, failed, or unavailable. New Protect authorization is denied server-side for Free tenants and allowed only after verified Pro entitlement; a later billing change never abandons an existing protected trial or blocks its Keep, cancellation, worker, or verification path. Replayed events are harmless, invalid signatures and unauthorized endpoints are rejected, past_due/unpaid/expired subscriptions do not retain Pro, cancellation at period end preserves entitlement only through the paid period, and no live-mode Stripe object is created or modified.
